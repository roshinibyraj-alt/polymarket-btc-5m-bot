# Polymarket BTC 5-minute momentum strategy

The demo bot polls a public CCXT BTC spot ticker every **500 ms** and compares the latest price with a sample about **1,000 ms earlier**. Its trigger adapts to volatility: it uses the **99th percentile of absolute one-second BTC moves** collected over a rolling window of up to **20 minutes**, with a **$1 minimum threshold**. After startup it waits for 120 valid move samples (about one minute at the default cadence); the baseline then grows to a full 20 minutes as data accumulates. The dashboard shows the current threshold and warm-up progress. Moves at or above the threshold target UP; moves at or below its negative target DOWN. No entry is allowed during the first **60 seconds** after a five-minute market window opens. While waiting, the latest signal remains active until an opposite signal. After the delay, the bot records at most one filled entry per five-minute window and buys only when the signaled side's best ask is **below $0.50**. Each BUY targets 500 shares, can sweep asks up to **50% above the observed best ask** by default (configurable with `MAX_BUY_SLIPPAGE_PERCENT`), and is capped at **$0.4999**; limited depth can produce a partial or absent fill. After entry, opposite signals do not sell or reopen; the position is held for settlement.

Coinbase `BTC/USD` is the default feed: it is reachable from this runtime and its CCXT rate limit permits 500 ms polling. Set `CCXT_EXCHANGE` and, if needed, `CCXT_SYMBOL` to select another CCXT market; the feed honors an exchange's higher minimum interval if it requires one. Binance returned a location restriction from this runtime, so Binance connectivity is not assumed. The dashboard reports feed errors and staleness; a missing or stale feed cannot generate a signal.

Demo marketable BUY orders use visible Polymarket book depth, require the signaled side's best ask to be below $0.50, and cap execution at $0.4999 even when the configured 50% slippage allowance is higher. Thin books within that cap can produce a partial or absent fill. At most one BUY order is sent per five-minute window; opposite signals after entry do not submit sells or new buys. Estimated crypto taker fees are included in demo cash and P&L. A held-side CLOB midpoint at or above **$0.99** is counted as a $1 payout per remaining share; a best bid at or below **$0.01** is counted as $0.00. This is a demo threshold heuristic, not official resolution. Other shares still open at the five-minute close wait for official Polymarket resolution. Account equity is cash plus the marked value of all open shares; total P&L is equity minus starting capital, and marks remain available when the active market rolls over.

This strategy is strictly demo-only. `LIVE_TRADING=true` is refused before wallet authentication, and the active bot uses only `DemoTrader`. The $10,000 paper balance and history reset when the process restarts. Simulated fills, estimated fees, and P&L are behavior checks—not evidence of live execution or profitability.

Run `npm test` from this directory to verify the feed, adaptive threshold, order behavior, and live guard. For local startup and dashboard checks, follow `DEMO_RUNBOOK.md`.
