Strategy and rules
Market scan: active, tradable non-sports, non-crypto markets whose listed end time is within two hours. Outcomes and token IDs come from Gamma; prices come from the CLOB WebSocket.
Entry: rest a 20-share paper limit buy at the current midpoint when the midpoint is $0.80–$0.90 and the best bid is above $0.60. The unfilled order is cancelled when midpoint leaves that band.
Fill model: confirm fills only from newly visible ask size at or below the resting limit. Partial fills are tracked; no taker entry fee or rebate is assumed.
Take-profit: rest a simulated limit sell at $0.99 and fill from visible bids at or above $0.99. Maker fees and rebates are not estimated.
Stop-loss: when best bid reaches $0.60 or lower, sweep available bids. That market is then removed from active scanning and will not be traded again.
Resolution: any shares left after an exit remain paper-open until Gamma reports the official result. Settlement uses the resolved outcome payout.
Safety: demo-only. No wallet or real-order code is loaded by this app.

Prices and fills are simulated from public order-book snapshots. A live market may have little or no size at the displayed price.
