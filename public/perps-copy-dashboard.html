How this paper copy works

Source: reads public Perps fills and portfolio snapshots for the selected owner EOA. It never signs or submits orders.

Proportional size: starting capital is $5,000. Each position targets master size × current paper equity ÷ current master equity. Existing positions are adjusted at each portfolio snapshot; sizes are rounded down to instrument precision.

Paper execution: simulates each change one tick worse than the reported source fill and applies the current tier-0 taker fee estimate (0.05%).

No fixed notional cap: the bot matches the master’s effective leverage, without an extra margin reserve. Notional can exceed paper equity. Adjustments incur simulated fees and slippage, so matching is approximate after costs and rounding.

Limitations: funding payments and market impact beyond one tick are not included. This is a simulator, not a promise of live execution or profitability.
