Strategy rules
  • Use Binance BTCUSDT one-minute kline WebSocket candles. No direction or signal comes from Polymarket prices or trade pressure.
  • At the start of minutes 1–3, use the immediately previous closed Binance candle. Minute 1 uses the previous window’s final candle; minutes 2–3 use the preceding candle in the current window. Minutes 4–5 are skipped.
  • A previous RED candle buys 200 UP shares; a previous GREEN candle buys 200 DOWN shares. The current candle’s color does not affect that minute’s entry. Doji or unavailable previous candles do not create a directional entry.
  • Only minutes 1–3 can trigger one buy each. Minutes 4–5 never enter. Candle color changes never sell positions.
  • At a $0.99 midpoint, take profit by crediting $1/share only if the position will not close at a net loss after fees. There is no stop-loss; positions not closed by take-profit stay open for official resolution.
  • Paper taker-style buys use visible CLOB depth and modeled fees; shallow books can partially fill.
