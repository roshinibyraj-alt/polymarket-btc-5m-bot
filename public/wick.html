Signals use the previous completed candle: green → UP, red → DOWN; a doji follows two or more consecutive prior red/green candles in the opposite direction. Each signal places 500-share limits at $0.30 and $0.20; unfilled orders cancel at 180 seconds on 5m windows and 540 seconds on 15m windows. A fresh own-side best bid at $0.99 or $0.01 completes the whole window, closes remaining shares at their own bid or $0, and cancels pending entries. Open shares close at rollover. No official outcomes are used. Paper ledgers reset when the process restarts. No live orders are submitted.
