Paper-only strategy: monitor UP and DOWN independently using Polymarket midpoint history. A side may buy 20 shares when it has fallen at least $0.04 from the reference high over the last 20 seconds, then rebounds at least $0.01 from a recent trough with two rising quotes. Maximum four entries per side per 5-minute window, at least 10 seconds apart; that side must recover $0.02 from its last trigger before it rearms. Buy taker sweeps are capped at $0.85. Through second 240, sell all open shares on a side only when the visible bid sweep is at least $0.05 above weighted entry and remains profitable after modeled fees. After second 240, make no strategy trades; unsold shares wait for official resolution. The demo runner does not load real-order modules. This experimental approach is not a claim of profitability.
