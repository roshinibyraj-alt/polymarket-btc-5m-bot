Current strategy

Trade only when the estimated edge survives fees and execution costs.

Each entry sweeps the visible asks for up to 10 shares. A FAK-style partial fill is recorded only when at least 5 shares and $1 notional execute; any remainder is cancelled. No resting-order or touch-fill assumptions, no forced churn, and no live order module.
