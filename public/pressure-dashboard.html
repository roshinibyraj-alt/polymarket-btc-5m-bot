How this paper strategy works

These rules are visible so each decision can be audited.

  • BTC 5-minute windows only. Binance spot and the Polymarket CLOB provide live inputs.
  • Sample aggressive buy/sell pressure once per second; evaluate every 15 seconds.
  • Estimate resolution direction from BTC’s move from the window open, recent pressure, closed candles, and Polymarket midpoints.
  • Use a 50-share visible-ask sweep only when the estimate is at least 58%, full-size depth is available, and estimated net edge after taker fees is at least $0.05/share.
  • UP and DOWN positions are independent. The model may add 50 shares on each qualifying review.
  • Never sell at an estimated net loss. A losing side stays open while an opposite side can enter; sell only when the model favors an exit and the visible-bid sweep is profitable after fees. No fixed TP or SL.
  • Any unsold position goes to official resolution of the market. Live order execution is disabled.
PAPER ONLY. Probabilities are experimental and uncalibrated; simulated fills depend on visible depth and modeled fees.
